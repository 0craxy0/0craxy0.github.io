Introducing Aether

Running a script should be the easy part. You shouldn’t have to trade performance for compatibility, lose your setup when you change games, or guess what went wrong.

Aether brings it all into one loader. Paste a single line and the whole interface arrives: a library that stays current, modules that switch on as you need them, and every setting remembered the next time you join.
